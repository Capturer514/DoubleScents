Our Founder

The founder, Muhammad Haikal bin Mohd Zaki, had a difficult start in his career. He lived in a small house with his parents in the coast of Malaysia, where they were disappointed with him joining art school, thinking it wouldn't land him any opportunities. Instead, he started his perfume journey in Paris, where was introduced to the city's home for fragrances.

He then socialized and communicated with various artists and artisans, including Christian Dior and Marcel Herrand, who are famous celebrities in Paris. One of these people invited him and introduced him to fragrances into their workplace. There, he became inspired to work on fragrances and begun to hone his craft.

Muhammad Haikal became deeply invested and was highly passionate towards fragrances, as he begun to discover and utilize flowers, minerals and other scents that can help him produce amazing fragrances. He would also make contact with other international and local fragrance brands and conglomerates in order to build relationships and establish businesses. Today, DoubleScents© is considered one of the most successful and popular fragrance shops in Malaysia and throughout the world.
