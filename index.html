About DoubleScents©

More than 15 years has DoubleScents sold their perfumes, men and women included, for prices that are considered cheap. Our stock mainly consists of luxury European brands spanning more than 100 that includes Dior, Chanel, Paco Rabanne, Jean-Paul Gauthier and Gucci etc.


Our main business not only focuses on stocking and selling fragrances, but we also have fragrance scholars that assist in our business as well. Our employees are well knowledged in marketing and sales found within the perfume industry. We have a large set workers skilled in fragrance knowledge.


Having been nominated for the Fragrance Foundation Online Retailer of the Year since last year, we are willingly able to offer a wrap, an engraving of a bottle and user personalized services across our shop. Also, you can recycle a bottle of perfume for our store and receive a bonus 10% discount for your next purchase!
